Below are a few new scenes I’ve made with Terragen 2. They’re works in progress, but I thought I would share them anyway :) 

The first two are my first attempts at postprocessing and it has not gone well. The original was really washed out so I was trying to liven it up a little and I think the green in both ended up _too_ green.

The third one I used to test how many objects I could have in a scene at one time. All the blades of grass are individual objects (they create a population in Terragen terms). I think if I better matched the background “grass” surface color to the color of the blades of grass, this one would look pretty good.

The final one I was messing around with shaders and more populations. I’m not sure what exactly is wrong with this one, but it feels fake. I tried to recreate a treeline scene, but maybe the lighting isn’t right or something.
