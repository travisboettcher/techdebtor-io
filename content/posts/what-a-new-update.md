This update is a long time coming, and will probably be much less impressive than I hope it is.

Since my last post, so much has happened: I went to XSEDE13 in the lovely San Diego; I was hired at a transportation company as a programmer in the IS department; and I moved! Oh, and I started my last semester of college!

The XSEDE13 conference was a lot of fun. I flew into California on the closing day of Comic-Con, and although I wasn’t able to participate in that, I did get to see a lot of the cosplay :) During the conference itself, I enjoyed a couple tutorials, volunteered at the front desk and several talks, and vied for dominance in a programming competition (which my team totally won!). One of the most fun things I got to do was have dinner on the USS Midway, a decommissioned aircraft carrier! We got to take several tours and socialize with many of the people at the conference.

The week after returning from California, I started my full-time job as a programmer! It’s for a national trucking company and one of the hardest things to learn isn’t the programming language they use (although that hasn’t been the most fun); instead it’s learning the business logic, rules, and policy that has been the most difficult. I suppose that is true for any new job in an area you previously knew very little about.

Finally, because my new job was almost 50 minutes away, we moved! I still have a 35 minute commute, but that seems much more reasonable. And now with school in full swing, those extra 30 minutes each day can go toward studying! 😛

That’s about it for now. Look for future posts about the [RenderFarm] project – I am still working on that this semester and hope to have a working frontend and Maya integration done by the end of the semester.
