This site uses [Plausible Analytics](https://plausible.io/) to understand how many
people visit and which posts they read.

Plausible is designed to be privacy-friendly:

- It doesn't use cookies.
- It doesn't collect or store any personal data, and visitors aren't tracked across
  sites or devices.
- It only reports aggregate stats — things like page views, referring sites, and
  approximate location by country — and no individual visitor can be identified from
  that data.

No consent banner is shown because none is required: nothing personal is collected,
so there's nothing to opt in or out of.

If you have questions, feel free to reach out.
